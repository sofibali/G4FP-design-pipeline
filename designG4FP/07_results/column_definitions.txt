G4FP Design Pipeline - Column Definitions
==========================================

Columns in: 06_ligand_state_comparison_results.csv
          and 07_final_candidates.csv


IDENTIFIERS
-----------
seq_id                      Design sequence number within its template (0-99)
template                    Template structure name (e.g. G4FP_des1_cro_mod0)
global_id                   Unique ID: {template}_design_{seq_id} (07 only)
is_template                 True if this row is the template sequence itself
                            (processed as a pseudo-design for reference)


SEED COUNTS
-----------
n_holo_seeds                Number of AF3 seed replicates analyzed for holo state (typ. 25)
                            Units: count (dimensionless)
n_apo_seeds                 Number of AF3 seed replicates analyzed for apo state (typ. 25)
                            Units: count (dimensionless)


HOLO (BOUND) STATE METRICS — mean across 25 seeds
--------------------------------------------------
holo_ptm                    Predicted TM-score: overall fold quality
                            Units: dimensionless (0-1, higher = better fold)
holo_ptm_sd                 Standard deviation of pTM across seeds
                            Units: dimensionless
holo_iptm                   Interface pTM: protein-DNA interface quality
                            Units: dimensionless (0-1, higher = better binding)
holo_iptm_sd                Standard deviation of iPTM across seeds
                            Units: dimensionless
holo_ranking_score          AF3 ranking score (composite confidence metric)
                            Units: dimensionless (0-1)
holo_ranking_score_sd       SD of ranking score across seeds
                            Units: dimensionless
holo_fraction_disordered    Fraction of residues predicted disordered
                            Units: dimensionless (0-1, lower = more ordered)
holo_fraction_disordered_sd SD of fraction disordered across seeds
                            Units: dimensionless
holo_mean_plddt             Mean per-residue confidence (pLDDT) for chain A
                            Units: pLDDT score (0-100, higher = more confident)
holo_mean_plddt_sd          SD of mean pLDDT across seeds
                            Units: pLDDT score
holo_chromophore_plddt      Mean pLDDT of chromophore residues TYG (residues 197-199) in holo
                            Units: pLDDT score (0-100)
holo_chromophore_plddt_sd   SD of chromophore pLDDT across seeds
                            Units: pLDDT score
holo_mean_pae               Mean predicted aligned error
                            Units: Angstroms (lower = better)
holo_mean_pae_sd            SD of mean PAE across seeds
                            Units: Angstroms
holo_interface_pae          Mean PAE between protein and DNA chains
                            Units: Angstroms (lower = better interface)
holo_interface_pae_sd       SD of interface PAE across seeds
                            Units: Angstroms


APO (UNBOUND) STATE METRICS — mean across 25 seeds
---------------------------------------------------
apo_ptm                     Predicted TM-score for apo (protein only, no DNA)
                            Units: dimensionless (0-1)
apo_ptm_sd                  SD of apo pTM across seeds
                            Units: dimensionless
apo_ranking_score           AF3 ranking score for apo state
                            Units: dimensionless (0-1)
apo_ranking_score_sd        SD of apo ranking score across seeds
                            Units: dimensionless
apo_fraction_disordered     Fraction disordered in apo (higher = more unfolded = good for switch)
                            Units: dimensionless (0-1)
apo_fraction_disordered_sd  SD of apo fraction disordered across seeds
                            Units: dimensionless
apo_mean_plddt              Mean pLDDT in apo state (lower = less confident = good for switch)
                            Units: pLDDT score (0-100)
apo_mean_plddt_sd           SD of apo mean pLDDT across seeds
                            Units: pLDDT score
apo_chromophore_plddt       Mean pLDDT of chromophore TYG in apo state
                            Units: pLDDT score (0-100)
apo_chromophore_plddt_sd    SD of apo chromophore pLDDT across seeds
                            Units: pLDDT score
apo_mean_pae                Mean PAE in apo state
                            Units: Angstroms
apo_mean_pae_sd             SD of apo mean PAE across seeds
                            Units: Angstroms


BACKWARD-COMPATIBLE ALIASES
----------------------------
chromophore_holo_plddt      Same as holo_chromophore_plddt
chromophore_holo_plddt_sd   Same as holo_chromophore_plddt_sd
chromophore_apo_plddt       Same as apo_chromophore_plddt
chromophore_apo_plddt_sd    Same as apo_chromophore_plddt_sd


HOLO vs APO DIFFERENCES
------------------------
mean_plddt_diff             holo_mean_plddt - apo_mean_plddt
                            Positive = holo more confident than apo (good)
                            Units: pLDDT score (delta)
mean_plddt_diff_sd          Propagated SD: sqrt(holo_sd^2 + apo_sd^2)
                            Units: pLDDT score
chromophore_plddt_diff      holo_chromophore_plddt - apo_chromophore_plddt
                            Positive = chromophore more stable in holo (good)
                            Units: pLDDT score (delta)
chromophore_plddt_diff_sd   Propagated SD of chromophore pLDDT difference
                            Units: pLDDT score
pae_diff                    holo_mean_pae - apo_mean_pae
                            Units: Angstroms (delta)
pae_diff_sd                 Propagated SD of PAE difference
                            Units: Angstroms


STRUCTURAL COMPARISON (HOLO vs APO)
------------------------------------
global_rmsd                 Mean Calpha RMSD between matched holo-apo seed pairs
                            Reference: each holo seed superimposed onto its
                            matched apo seed (same seed index). NOT vs template.
                            Higher = more structural change between bound/unbound (good)
                            Units: Angstroms
global_rmsd_sd              SD of global RMSD across the 25 matched seed pairs
                            Units: Angstroms
chromophore_rmsd            Mean RMSD of chromophore residues (197-199) between
                            matched holo-apo seed pairs
                            Units: Angstroms
chromophore_rmsd_sd         SD of chromophore RMSD across matched seed pairs
                            Units: Angstroms
mean_per_res_rmsd           Same as global_rmsd (kept for compatibility)
                            Units: Angstroms
max_per_res_rmsd            Maximum RMSD observed across the 25 matched seed pairs
                            Units: Angstroms


SEQUENCE (07 only)
------------------
sequence                    Full protein sequence (with TYG chromophore inserted)
                            Units: amino acid one-letter codes


RELATIVE APO STABILITY (07 only)
---------------------------------
apo_plddt_vs_template       Design apo pLDDT minus template apo pLDDT
                            Negative = design is less stable than template in apo (good)
                            Positive = design is more stable than template in apo (bad)
                            Units: pLDDT score (delta)


FITNESS SCORE COMPONENTS (07 only) — each normalized 0-1
--------------------------------------------------------
score_component_chrom_holo  Normalized chromophore holo pLDDT (weight: 25%)
                            Higher = chromophore more confident when bound
                            Units: dimensionless (0-1, normalized)
score_component_iptm        Normalized iPTM (weight: 25%)
                            Higher = better protein-DNA interface
                            Units: dimensionless (0-1, normalized)
score_component_rmsd        Normalized global RMSD (weight: 15%)
                            Higher = more structural change holo vs apo
                            Units: dimensionless (0-1, normalized)
score_component_holo_ptm    Normalized holo pTM (weight: 15%)
                            Higher = better overall fold quality
                            Units: dimensionless (0-1, normalized)
score_component_rel_apo     Normalized relative apo pLDDT (weight: 10%, inverted)
                            Higher score = lower apo stability vs template
                            Units: dimensionless (0-1, normalized)
score_component_confidence  Normalized reproducibility penalty (weight: 5%)
                            Higher = lower SD across seeds (more reproducible)
                            Units: dimensionless (0-1, normalized)
score_component_chrom_diff  Normalized chromophore pLDDT difference (weight: 5%)
                            Higher = bigger chromophore stability gain in holo
                            Units: dimensionless (0-1, normalized)

fitness_score               Weighted sum of all components above
                            Higher = better G4FP candidate
                            Units: dimensionless (0-1)


SELECTION FLAGS (07 only)
-------------------------
in_pareto                   True if design is on the Pareto frontier
                            (non-dominated across iPTM, chromophore pLDDT, global RMSD)
in_top10_per_tpl            True if design is in the top 10 for its template (by fitness)
in_top50_global             True if design is in the top 50 overall (by fitness)
final_rank                  Rank among all scored designs (1 = best fitness score)
                            Units: integer rank


HARD FILTERS APPLIED BEFORE SELECTION (07)
------------------------------------------
Designs must pass all of:
  - holo_mean_plddt >= 70     (pLDDT score)
  - holo_ptm >= 0.5           (dimensionless)
  - holo_iptm >= 0.3          (dimensionless)
Designs failing these are still in the CSV but excluded from
top10/top50/Pareto selection pools.


================================================================================
Columns in: 07_ligandmpnn_entropy.csv
================================================================================

This file contains per-design LigandMPNN sampling entropy and confidence
metrics. Entropy is computed from the sampling probability distributions
output by LigandMPNN during sequence design. It quantifies how "decisive"
the model was at each position — low entropy means the model strongly
preferred one amino acid, high entropy means multiple amino acids were
similarly probable.

Merge with 07_final_candidates.csv on the 'global_id' column.

Source data: output_G4FP_*/01_ligandmpnn/stats/*.pt
  - sampling_probs tensor: (10000 designs x N_residues x 20 amino acids)
  - Each value is the probability the model assigned to each amino acid
    at each position for each design
  - Only the 100 filtered designs (top 1% by confidence) are in the CSV

IDENTIFIERS
-----------
global_id                       Unique ID matching 07_final_candidates.csv
                                Format: {template}_design_{seq_id}
template                        Template structure name
seq_id                          Design index within template (0-99)
                                Units: integer index


ENTROPY METRICS
---------------
All entropy values are Shannon entropy computed in base 2.
Units: bits
Range: 0 (deterministic, model chose exactly one AA) to
       log2(20) = 4.32 bits (uniform over all 20 amino acids)

mean_entropy_all                Mean entropy across ALL residue positions
                                Includes both designed and fixed positions
                                Units: bits
                                Typical values: 0.05-0.30 bits

mean_entropy_designed           Mean entropy across the 92 DESIGNED positions only
                                (residues A15-60 and A240-285)
                                This is the key metric — measures how confident
                                LigandMPNN was in its sequence choices
                                Lower = model was more decisive = stronger signal
                                Higher = model explored more AA options = less certain
                                Units: bits
                                Typical values: 0.15-0.30 bits

mean_entropy_fixed              Mean entropy at FIXED (non-designed) positions
                                Should be very low (~0) since these positions were
                                constrained to the template sequence
                                Values >0 indicate slight model uncertainty even
                                at fixed positions
                                Units: bits

mean_entropy_chromophore_region  Mean entropy in a +/-5 residue window around the
                                chromophore (TYG, residues 192-204)
                                Important because positions near the chromophore
                                affect fluorescence; lower entropy here means
                                LigandMPNN found strong sequence preferences
                                for chromophore packing
                                Units: bits

max_entropy                     Highest entropy at any single position
                                Identifies the most uncertain position in the design
                                Units: bits

min_entropy_designed            Lowest entropy among designed positions
                                Identifies the most confidently placed residue
                                in the designed region
                                Units: bits

n_designed_positions            Number of positions that were redesigned (92)
                                Units: count (dimensionless)
n_total_positions               Total protein length (varies by template, 284-306)
                                Units: count (residues)


LIGANDMPNN CONFIDENCE SCORES
-----------------------------
overall_confidence              LigandMPNN's mean log-probability across all
                                positions (higher = model is more confident in
                                the entire sequence). This was the primary metric
                                used for filtering top 1% (100 of 10K designs)
                                Units: mean log-probability (dimensionless, higher = better)

ligand_confidence               LigandMPNN's confidence specifically at positions
                                near the ligand (G4-DNA and CRO chromophore)
                                Higher = better predicted packing around ligands
                                Units: mean log-probability (dimensionless)

seq_rec                         Sequence recovery: fraction of designed positions
                                that match the template sequence
                                Higher = more conservative design (closer to WT)
                                Lower = more novel design (more mutations)
                                Units: fraction (0-1, dimensionless)

combined_confidence             Average of overall_confidence and ligand_confidence
                                Used as secondary ranking criterion
                                Units: mean log-probability (dimensionless)


================================================================================
Columns in: 07_ligandmpnn_positional_entropy.csv
================================================================================

Aligned grid of template sequences with per-position mean entropy.
All 10 template sequences are aligned to the longest template using
pairwise Needleman-Wunsch alignment. Each alignment column has the
residue identity and mean entropy (across all 10,000 LigandMPNN designs)
for each template.

COLUMNS
-------
alignment_column                Alignment position (1-indexed)
                                Units: integer index

{template_short}_residue        Amino acid at this alignment position for this
                                template, or '-' for a gap
                                Units: amino acid one-letter code

{template_short}_entropy        Shannon entropy averaged across all 10K designs
                                at this position for this template
                                High = position is variable across designs
                                Low = position is conserved (structurally critical)
                                NaN = gap (no residue at this alignment position)
                                Units: bits (base 2)

{template_short}_designed       True if this position was in the design region
                                (residues A15-60 or A240-285), False if fixed

(template_short examples: des1_cro_mod0, r20_l1_cro_mod0, r28_l2_cro_mod3)

INTERPRETATION
--------------
- Fixed positions (designed=False) should have near-zero entropy
- Designed positions with LOW entropy (<0.1 bits): model strongly prefers one AA
  -> likely structurally critical (core packing, H-bonds)
- Designed positions with HIGH entropy (>0.5 bits): model sees multiple good options
  -> likely solvent-exposed or tolerant to substitution
- Positions near the chromophore (197-199) with low entropy suggest the model
  found strong sequence-structure relationships for fluorescence


================================================================================
Columns in: template_af3_metrics.csv
================================================================================

Per-template AF3 prediction metrics computed from 25 seed replicates each
for bound (holo) and apo states. These serve as the baseline reference —
designs are compared against their parent template's metrics.

Generated by: analyze_template_af3.py

IDENTIFIERS
-----------
template_name                   Template structure name (G4FP_*)


FITNESS SCORE
-------------
fitness_score                   Fitness score computed with same weights as designs
                                (for comparison only — templates use rel_apo=0.5
                                since they are their own reference)
                                Units: dimensionless (0-1)


HOLO/APO METRICS (same units as 06/07 columns above)
-----------------------------------------------------
holo_mean_plddt                 Template holo mean pLDDT (mean across 25 seeds)
                                Units: pLDDT score (0-100)
holo_mean_plddt_sd              SD across 25 seeds
                                Units: pLDDT score
holo_chromophore_plddt          Template chromophore pLDDT in holo
                                Units: pLDDT score (0-100)
holo_chromophore_plddt_sd       SD
                                Units: pLDDT score
holo_ptm                        Template holo pTM
                                Units: dimensionless (0-1)
holo_iptm                       Template holo iPTM (protein-DNA interface)
                                Units: dimensionless (0-1)
holo_ranking_score              Template holo AF3 ranking score
                                Units: dimensionless (0-1)
holo_fraction_disordered        Template holo fraction disordered
                                Units: dimensionless (0-1)
holo_interface_pae              Template holo interface PAE
                                Units: Angstroms
holo_mean_pae                   Template holo mean PAE
                                Units: Angstroms
apo_mean_plddt                  Template apo mean pLDDT
                                Units: pLDDT score (0-100)
apo_chromophore_plddt           Template apo chromophore pLDDT
                                Units: pLDDT score (0-100)
apo_ptm                         Template apo pTM
                                Units: dimensionless (0-1)
apo_ranking_score               Template apo AF3 ranking score
                                Units: dimensionless (0-1)
apo_fraction_disordered         Template apo fraction disordered
                                Units: dimensionless (0-1)
apo_mean_pae                    Template apo mean PAE
                                Units: Angstroms
(all with corresponding _sd columns in the same units)

n_holo_seeds                    Number of holo seeds analyzed (25)
                                Units: count
n_apo_seeds                     Number of apo seeds analyzed (25)
                                Units: count


HOLO-APO COMPARISON
-------------------
global_rmsd                     Mean Calpha RMSD between matched holo-apo seed pairs
                                Units: Angstroms
global_rmsd_sd                  SD across seed pairs
                                Units: Angstroms
chromophore_rmsd                Chromophore RMSD between holo-apo pairs
                                Units: Angstroms
chromophore_rmsd_sd             SD
                                Units: Angstroms
mean_plddt_diff                 holo_mean_plddt - apo_mean_plddt
                                Units: pLDDT score (delta)
mean_plddt_diff_sd              Propagated SD
                                Units: pLDDT score
chromophore_plddt_diff          holo_chromophore_plddt - apo_chromophore_plddt
                                Units: pLDDT score (delta)
