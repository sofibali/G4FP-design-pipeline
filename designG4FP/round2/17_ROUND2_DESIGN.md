# Round 2: a new design campaign

**Status: planning. Nothing in this document has been run.**

Supersedes the scenario framing in `15_ROUND2_CAMPAIGN_PLAN.md`, which treated
round 2 as an iteration over round-1 designs. That document remains valid as the
cost model and the regrouping record; its scenarios A / B / B-lite are withdrawn.

---

## 1. What round 2 is

Round 2 builds new designs from new scaffolds. **Round 1's designs are not the
starting point. Round 1's measurements are.**

The reason is not taste. It is that an iteration cannot be forecast and a fresh
round can. Round 1 contains no parent/child pairs, so the inheritance of gate
behaviour from a parent design to a redesigned child has never been measured
here; every iteration forecast in `15_round2_forecast.csv` therefore carries a
band spanning a factor of 8.5. A fresh round needs no inheritance assumption at
all, because round 1 *is* a measurement of a fresh round: 999 designs in, 71 out.
Its band spans a factor of 1.8, and the only extrapolated term in it is the
CONTACT uplift from the wider design window.

The second reason is that the two round-1 designs that actually fluoresced are
rejected by the round-1 cascade (`design_0072` at BIND, `design_0016` at SWITCH).
Iterating the survivors means iterating a set that is, on the only evidence we
have, anti-correlated with the phenotype we want.

## 2. What round 1 established, and what each one constrains

| # | Measured in round 1 | Constraint on round 2 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **70.2 % of designs die at CONTACT** — AF3 predicts fewer than 3 protein–G4 contacts. Not a bad interface; no interface. | The interface must be designed, not hoped for. Design window 40+40 (G4-contact coverage 140 → 251 residues). This is the single highest-yield change available. |
| 2 | **The library is four templates wide, not ten.** `des1_cro_mod4` gives 47 of 71 survivors; four templates give zero. | Scaffold *diversity* did not buy diversity of outcome. Generate fewer scaffolds and vet each one before committing 100 designs to it. |
| 3 | **G4 binders are rigid as a class** — Nb55 0.83 Å, DARPin 2E4 0.75–1.02 Å apo-vs-holo, two unrelated scaffolds. Flat end-stacking on a terminal tetrad selects for a pre-organised flat surface. | Stop looking for induced fit in the binding module. The switch must come from somewhere else: a hinge the module is mounted on, or the dye itself. |
| 4 | **The cascade does not select for chromophore maturation**, and maturation is where 19 of 21 tested designs failed. 10 of 21 passed all four gates; all 10 were dark. | Maturation must be gated separately — or deleted. See §3.2. |
| 5 | **Round 1 was never predicted against the Myc G4.** All ten templates used the 16-nt `GGGTGGGTGGGTGGGT`. | The target is a round-2 input, not an inheritance. Every percentile threshold in `13_RESCORING_PLAN.md` is calibrated against the 16-nt target and does not transfer unchanged. |

A sixth result constrains how round 2 is *read* rather than designed: rescoring
metrics do not rank. Boltz-2 ipSAE scored below a coin flip on 30 measured
binders. Round 2 should use gates and diversity to choose what to order, not a
composite fitness score to rank it.

## 3. Three decisions that precede any compute

None of these is settled by the budget. All three must be made before a single
scaffold is generated, because each one invalidates work done under the other.

### 3.1 The target

16-nt `GGGTGGGTGGGTGGGT` (what round 1 actually used, so thresholds transfer) or
the 22-nt Myc G4 (what the round-2 documents assume, so thresholds must be
recalibrated). Choosing Myc also changes the module shortlist.

### 3.2 Maturation: gate it or delete it

- **Gate it.** Requires a computational predictor of chromophore maturation.
  This tree contains none, and calibrating one needs data that does not exist —
  2 positives out of 21 is not a training set.
- **Delete it.** Bind a dye whose fluorescence is switched by its environment
  rather than generated by the protein. The rhodamine lactone ⇌ zwitterion
  equilibrium is the concrete version (Option E, `g4fp-degrado-vdm-route`).
  There is nothing to mature, so the 19/21 failure mode cannot recur.

Deleting it is the only one of the two that can start now.

### 3.3 Where the switch lives

From `16_G4_MODULE_SURVEY.md`, measured from deposited coordinates:

- **Nb55 / DARPin-class module.** Rigid (≤1.0 Å). Switch must come from the dye.
  Pairs naturally with 3.2-delete.
- **Rap1 DBD.** Two rigid blocks on a hinge at residues ~440–460; across the
  hinge, residues move up to 35.7 Å with a 104° rotation, while the recognition
  helix itself stays rigid at 0.46 Å. The one natural module with real amplitude.
  Caveat, stated plainly: this is duplex-vs-G4, not apo-vs-G4 — no apo structure
  exists — so it is a *structure-discrimination* switch, not an occupancy switch,
  and that is not what ΔiPAE measures.
- **Nucleolin RBD12.** The selectivity option; reads the central loop and 5′
  flank. Costs all four RBDs for affinity.

The slot will host any of them: measured graft envelope is Rg 13.5 Å, span
32.3 Å, attachment points 5.2 Å apart, which a ~105-residue folded domain
matches almost exactly.

## 4. The pipeline

Unchanged in mechanism from round 1; changed in what each stage is asked to do.

| Step | What runs | What changed from round 1 |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Fix target, module, switch location (§3) | new — round 1 inherited all three implicitly |
| 1 | Generate scaffolds around module + dye pocket | new scaffolds, not round-1 backbones |
| 2 | LigandMPNN, 10k sequences per scaffold, ligand context on | design window 40+40, not the round-1 window |
| 3 | Keep top 1 % (100 per scaffold) | unchanged |
| 4 | AF3, apo and holo | unchanged |
| 5 | Gate: CONTACT → NEGATIVE → BIND → SWITCH | thresholds recalibrated if the target changes (§3.1) |
| 6 | Order ≥32 | round 1 ordered 21, which was underpowered — see §6 |

The gates are non-compensatory and ordered by how much each one kills, cheapest
and deadliest first. CONTACT stays in front because it removes 70 % of the
population.

## 5. Cost

From `17_forecast_denovo.py` / `17_forecast_denovo.csv`. AF3 anchors measured on
this tree (141.6 s holo, 138.2 s apo, 2 GPUs); the model reproduces round 1's
measured 40.6 h to within −4.2 %.

| | scaffolds | designs | AF3 preds | wall clock | survivors |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **P** — pilot | 2 | 40 | 80 | **2.7 h** | 3–5 |
| **N1** — narrow, one module | 4 | 400 | 800 | **18.4 h** | 28–51 |
| **N2** — round-1 width | 10 | 1,000 | 2,000 | **46.1 h** | 71–127 |
| **N3** — wide, two modules | 20 | 2,000 | 4,000 | **3.8 d** | 142–255 |

Survivor bands: low end applies the round-1 funnel unchanged (7.11 %, which
reconciles exactly to 71/999); high end applies the 40+40 CONTACT uplift
(12.74 %).

**Scaffold generation is not costed here.** Round 1's ten templates were produced
before the step this repo times, so those hours are additive to every row.

For comparison, iterating the 71 round-1 survivors costs 308 h (12.8 d) for a
survivor count between 505 and 4,276. A full new round at the same width as
round 1 costs 46 h. **The new round is 6.7× cheaper and its forecast is 4.7×
narrower.**

## 6. What the compute does not decide

Order size. 0 of 21 G4-responsive excludes a true switch rate above 13.3 % at
95 % confidence, but below 3.2 % seeing nothing was more likely than not even if
the designs worked. For 80 % power: 53 constructs at a 3 % true rate, 32 at 5 %,
16 at 10 %, 9 at the 17 % Chang & Polizzi measured. Round 1 ordered 21.

N2 and N3 clear 32 survivors at the low end of the band. N1 clears it only if the
window uplift is real. Every scenario fits inside a week of GPU time — **compute
was never the limiting resource, and it is not the reason round 1 could not tell
a working design from a dead one.**
